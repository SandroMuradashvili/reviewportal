# ReviewPortal Legal and Policy Requirements

> Working product specification, not legal advice. A Georgian-qualified lawyer must review the final Georgian version and confirm consumer, privacy, electronic-commerce, tax, marketing, and cross-border requirements before launch. English and Russian texts should be translations of the lawyer-approved controlling version.

## Required public documents

ReviewPortal should publish separate, versioned pages for:

1. Terms of Service for business owners.
2. Privacy Policy covering owners, visitors, and private-feedback submitters.
3. Acceptable Use Policy.
4. Cookie/analytics notice and consent controls where required.
5. Product sales, delivery, returns, and warranty terms once physical sales are offered.
6. Subscription, activation, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms.
7. Copyright/DMCA-style contact process appropriate to the jurisdictions served.
8. Data-subject request and account-deletion instructions.

Do not add an EULA unless downloadable/licensed client software is introduced; a web SaaS generally needs Terms of Service instead. Do not copy a US DMCA notice as if it automatically governs a Georgian service—counsel should define the correct takedown process and agent/contact details.

## Terms of Service content

- Identify the legal operator, registered/contact address, support contact, effective date, controlling language, and governing law/forum.
- Define owner eligibility, accurate account information, credential security, authorized business representation, and the five-portal limit.
- Explain the ten-submission trial, what counts as a submission, what happens at the limit, manual activation, package duration, expiry, and suspension.
- State that WhatsApp conversations are inquiries, not automatic orders, payments, subscriptions, reservations, or activations.
- Define fees, taxes, agreed payment method, invoice/receipt handling, renewal method, cancellation, refunds, and service consequences. Cancellation must be no harder than activation.
- Explain acceptable content, lawful customer solicitation, ownership/licensing of business logos and copy, and responsibility for configured destinations.
- Reserve proportionate rights to pause abusive, illegal, unsafe, misleading, or platform-violating portals, with notice/appeal where appropriate.
- Disclaim guaranteed rating, ranking, revenue, review volume, or business outcomes. Explain that scans, unique visitors, redirects, and private feedback are estimates/events, not verified public reviews.
- Address service availability, maintenance, force majeure, limitation of liability, indemnity only where enforceable, termination, data export/deletion, changes to terms, and notices.
- Avoid clauses that waive non-waivable Georgian consumer or data-protection rights.

## Privacy Policy content

- Describe exactly what is collected: owner identity/profile, authentication metadata, portal configuration, private ratings/comments, approximate visit/event data, product/package inquiries, support messages, legal acceptance, and security/audit records.
- State each purpose and legal basis confirmed by counsel: providing the service, security/fraud prevention, communications, legal compliance, and consent-based optional analytics/marketing where applicable.
- Explain that private feedback is provided to the relevant business owner and is not automatically a public review.
- Name or accurately categorize processors such as hosting, database, authentication, email, error monitoring, and WhatsApp/Meta when a user chooses that handoff. Link to relevant provider notices where appropriate.
- Explain international transfers, safeguards, retention periods/criteria, security measures at a responsible level, and who controls versus processes each data category.
- Provide access, correction, deletion, objection/restriction, portability, consent withdrawal, complaint, and contact procedures where applicable.
- Explain account and portal deletion, backup expiry, legal/security retention exceptions, and the real deletion timetable.
- Explain cookies/local storage and first-party analytics. Do not claim “no tracking” if visit tokens, analytics, authentication, or monitoring are used.
- Do not mention AI because v1 does not use AI features. If AI processing is later introduced, update the policy before activation.
- Never conceal third-party processors, retain data contrary to published promises, or make public a storage bucket containing content expected to be private.

## Acceptable Use Policy

Prohibit:

- Fake, incentivized, coerced, or misleading reviews and attempts to manipulate public-platform ratings.
- Selective solicitation that violates destination-platform rules.
- Impersonation of Google, ReviewPortal, another business, or an authority.
- Harassment, threats, hate, illegal content, malware, phishing, unsafe redirects, credential collection, and unauthorized personal data.
- Uploads or business branding the owner does not have rights to use.
- Automated abuse, scraping, service interference, trial circumvention, metric manipulation, and resale without permission.

Define reporting, investigation, evidence preservation, proportionate restriction, appeal, and repeat-violation termination procedures.

## Subscription and consumer fairness

- Show the full price, currency, duration, included portals/features, tax treatment, and whether renewal is automatic before agreement.
- Because v1 activation is manual, never imply instant activation. Record who activated the package and its effective/expiry dates.
- If automatic renewal or a free trial with later charging is introduced, obtain explicit informed consent, send advance reminders, provide a simple cancellation path, and comply with current Georgian requirements.
- Provide the same direct WhatsApp/support path for cancellation as for sign-up, plus an authenticated cancellation request option.
- Separate physical-product conditions from SaaS conditions: stock, delivery territory/time, inspection, returns, defects, warranty, and risk transfer need their own clear terms.

## Marketing and testimonial rules

- Use only real testimonials with documented permission and accurate context. Disclose incentives or material relationships.
- Never invent customer names, photos, logos, ratings, review counts, stock scarcity, discounts, countdowns, or outcome claims.
- Substantiate measurable claims and date statistics. Do not guarantee Google ranking or review growth.
- State clearly that ReviewPortal is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Google unless a genuine written relationship exists.
- Obtain permission before marketing to owners; do not repurpose anonymous customer feedback for advertising.

## Feedback handling and moderation

- Private comments should be visible only to the relevant owner and authorized ReviewPortal administrators when necessary for support, security, or legal compliance.
- Give owners status and internal-note tools, but do not imply that ReviewPortal validates the truth of feedback.
- Provide a channel to report threats, illegal content, personal data, or misdirected feedback and define a consistent moderation standard independent of sentiment/rating.
- Log administrative access and moderation actions. Do not silently alter customer comments.

## Legal acceptance and versioning

- Store document type, version, locale, user, timestamp, and acceptance action.
- Require fresh acceptance for material owner-contract changes; provide advance notice where required.
- Display effective and last-updated dates and preserve prior versions.
- Georgian counsel should decide which language controls and ensure the other versions are accurate and understandable.

## Pre-launch legal checklist

- Confirm legal entity/operator and complete contact details.
- Confirm Georgian governing law, consumer rights, privacy authority/contact, tax/invoicing, and cross-border transfer obligations.
- Approve Terms, Privacy Policy, Acceptable Use, cookies, subscription/cancellation, product returns/warranty, and takedown process.
- Confirm processor agreements and maintain a data/processor register.
- Verify UI behavior matches every promise about trial limits, activation, cancellation, retention, deletion, analytics, and stock.
- Complete native legal-language review for Georgian, English, and Russian.
- Re-review policies whenever payments, automated renewals, contact collection, SMS/email campaigns, AI, public testimonials, teams, or new review platforms are added.
